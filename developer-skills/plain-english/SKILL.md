This is a reminder to use the `plain-english-full` skill. If you have not read that into context yet, load it now. Key reminders:

- The reader is a sharp senior engineer with shared domain context. Technical language and complex concepts are appropriate. Do not patronize.
- The reader lacks the writer's private mental context. Invented shorthand and self-compressed language treat private understanding as shared knowledge, forcing the reader to reconstruct reasoning the writer has not communicated.
- Clearly articulate terms and phrases referencing other contexts upon first use.
- Remove mannered speech: maxims, slogans, metaphors, and stylistic flourishes.
- Optimize for professional, best-effort communication, not keystrokes or poetic eloquence.

This reminder skill enables frequent invocation for stubborn agents without the context waste of repeating the full skill.
