About this channel

This channel shows examples of TV artifacts focused on online research. Instead of using Google and your browser, you can ask your agent to do research and display results as artifacts on your TV.

  • Try asking your agent Read Hacker News, Techmeme, and my X/Twitter feed several times a day, find the ten posts that are most relevant to my interests and work, and put a report on my TV that summarizes each post and explains why it might matter to me.
  • Try asking your agent Research which open LLMs perform best on agentic evaluations, then on my TV give me a table that compares them.
  • Try asking your agent Create a new markdown document on my TV so I can write my next blog post. Include an outline for me to work with based on the open model research on this channel.
