Open model research

Deep research on open-weight LLMs for agentic workloads · Eval: AgentBench-2.0

Evaluated 8 open-weight models on AgentBench-2.0, a multi-task agentic evaluation suite covering tool-use orchestration, multi-step reasoning, code execution, and retrieval-augmented planning. Three models stand out for production agentic workloads: GLM-4.6-405B leads on tool-use orchestration, Llama-4-70B retains the strongest reasoning edge, and Mistral-Large-2 offers the best performance-per-parameter ratio. Smaller models (below 35B) struggle with multi-step tool chains but remain viable for single-step tasks.
