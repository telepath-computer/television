*How the promises in Navbar (UI) are proven.*

# Navbar (UI) — proof

Proves [specs/ui/app/top-bar/index.md](../../../../specs/ui/app/top-bar/index.md).

## Coverage model

The existing markup and browser-layout contracts keep their service mock: authored tabs, channels, notices, and widths enter the production navbar without requiring a server for every composition or pressure permutation. That mock forfeits real channel and tab delivery to the product and stage proofs cited below. A new markup table covers the collapsed lead, including the no-channels branch and the shared channel list and row menus; browser geometry contracts cover the lead floor under pressure and the switcher list and footer under constrained height without asserting exact pixel styling.

Switcher acceptance runs in a real browser against a running server under the [acceptance-test policy](../../../../specs/arch/testing-policy.md#^shape-acceptance). The selection walk uses the standing CSS-motion override, declares that mock, and makes no motion claim. Creation runs with real motion because the channel row grows into place; it waits on browser animation and focus signals rather than sleeps. Both walks use a browser-storage fixture to start collapsed, which supplies authored input to the production preference reader and replaces no mechanism. [Channel product acceptance](../../../product/channels.md#^ch-ac-switcher-focus-broadcast) owns the selected channel reaching a second client; the navbar proof owns this client, popover continuity, and input behavior.

The switcher markup contract requires a paired production popover without the `manual` attribute. Generic Escape and light dismissal, trigger ARIA maintenance, exclusivity, focus return, and lifecycle therefore stay with [popover lifecycle coverage](../../foundation/popover/index.md#^po-ac-lifecycle); outer panel sizing and placement stay with [popover placement coverage](../../foundation/popover/index.md#^po-ac-placement). The switcher browser contract separately proves that its list scrolls while its footer stays usable and stationary. The switcher also proves its own keyboard entry, arrows, Enter, and additional Space activation because it is deliberately not a menu and cannot inherit menu implementation coverage merely by citing [the menu interaction contract](../../foundation/menu/index.md#^mn-ac-input).

## Test hooks


`TopBarOptions.onRenderComplete` is the optional callback through which tests observe that the navbar's template has committed. The production app leaves it unset, so normal top-bar rendering has no completion callback.

> **Motion and assertion cost.** Cheap markup tables cover ordinary and collapsed composition. Controls continuity remains an identity check with a mutation record. Browser geometry is reserved for centering, collision avoidance, the usable band, the lead floor, immediate pressure response, and the one constrained switcher-overflow case. The pressure rows run without a motion override so a forbidden transition cannot be hidden; the overflow case may use the standing override because its outcome does not depend on motion. Switcher selection and creation share one real-browser/server setup, while creation and switcher drag coverage retain real row motion. Generic popover dismissal and outer placement are reused rather than repeated. Native window movement still earns the Electron seam because DOM markup and selector matching cannot prove it. Apart from the functional minimum drag bands, exact spacing, background, tone, target padding, child-control appearance, and the visual feel of centering remain staging and review concerns.


## Staging review

The fixed-height navbar requirement ([^tp-stable-height](../../../../specs/ui/app/top-bar/index.md#^tp-stable-height)) is checked in the composed app frame. Switching from a populated channel to the empty channel preserves navbar height, control position, and stage position in None and Clouds, in light and dark appearances. Chromium staging review on 2026-09-16 passed all four combinations. Exact height remains a styling review concern.

The [Channel popover frame](../../../../frames/app/channel-popover.frame) stages
pointer and keyboard navigation using the authoritative markup and CSS. Chromium
review on 2026-09-24 confirmed bounded arrow entry, pointer-to-keyboard handoff,
a single navigation fill without a row outline, persistent primary selection,
and Enter/Space selection under the behavior specified at that time. Selection now dismisses the popover under ^top-ac-switcher-select. The rename pose retains input focus
and arrow handling. Child-menu interaction is outside this preview.

Production navigation is covered by `packages/web/test/e2e/channel-switcher-context-menu.test.ts` “switcher navigation shares menu highlight and keeps persistent selection”, using real input and no motion override. It covers bounded entry, pointer-to-keyboard focus handoff, persistent selection, and keyboard reopening. The existing top-bar selection acceptance covers Enter and Space activation. Styling remains governed by the authoritative stylesheet copy and visual review.

The Channel popover preview also passed Chromium pointer checks for pinned reorder, pinning into a marked slot, outside unpinning back to the fixture creation order, preserved selection, continued popover visibility, Escape/pointer-cancel restoration, empty-group transitions, and held-edge scrolling in a constrained viewport. The corresponding production drag acceptance passed with an overflowing list, retained live artifact content, outside release, Escape, pointer cancellation, and capture loss. Shared sidebar acceptance retains the full group-state and interruption matrix.

A one-off inspection of the built production bundle on 2026-09-24 confirmed a neutral fill on the keyboard-focused row, no lingering fill under the stationary pointer, persistent primary selection, and no row outline. The sidebar, skills, and settings icons each measured 16px. A real drag beyond the panel remained visibly painted with the ordinary popover text colour and Unpin action; hit testing at the carried row confirmed that neither scroll container clipped it. These styling observations are review evidence, not additional permanent styling tests.
