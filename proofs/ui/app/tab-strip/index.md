*How the promises in Tab strip (UI) are proven.*

# Tab strip (UI) — proof

Proves [specs/ui/app/tab-strip/index.md](../../../../specs/ui/app/tab-strip/index.md).

## Coverage model

The suite exercises the production tab strip with authored lists and a recording application-service mock. Browser contracts retain compression, label-overflow updates, scrolling, keyboard, and drag-edge coverage; product proofs retain real application state and persistence. The markup contract now exercises the authored tab role and carried-state class. Coupled page motion remains with the Stage proof.

The [overflow-fade proof](../../../arch/ui/overflow-fade.md) owns helper geometry and lifecycle. Browser tests exercise the production tab strip with item masks at each edge in Chromium and Firefox. The view-disconnection test unmounts the real view and checks that retained tab nodes remain clear after subsequent DOM changes, proving that the view calls helper cleanup.

The compression assertion below uses the production floor constant. `test/repo/ui-measure-crossings.test.ts` checks that constant against [tab.floor_px](../../../../specs/ui/app/tab-strip/measures.yml#tab.floor_px), while `packages/web/test/foundation-copy.test.ts` checks the CSS copy against [tab.frame](../../../../specs/ui/app/tab-strip/tab.frame). Together with the browser assertion, these checks cover changes to the authored floor without duplicating its numeric value in a new test. Short labels retain their intrinsic width; visual review judges the resulting spacing and fade.

The shared-edge contract in `packages/web/test/e2e/window-edge-rims.test.ts` additionally uses the production tab-strip fixture to verify that disabling both decorative paints preserves selected-tab geometry and keyboard focus. It shares the application-service mock and limitations below.

## Assertions

### Test assertions

The suite samples the strip's meaningful markup states and proves its scrolling and keyboard behavior ([arch/testing-policy.md#^ui-suite-scope](../../../../specs/arch/testing-policy.md#^ui-suite-scope)). The rendered appearance of tab bounds, label truncation, edge fades, hover, and selected treatment stays authoritative in the linked templates and stylesheets and is judged in staging and review rather than tested for pixel conformance ([arch/testing-policy.md#^ui-styling-out](../../../../specs/arch/testing-policy.md#^ui-styling-out)). The DOM is only the instrument used to observe the behaviors below ([arch/testing-policy.md#^ui-dom-instrument](../../../../specs/arch/testing-policy.md#^ui-dom-instrument)).

Shared contract declaration for real-browser view behavior ([arch/testing-policy.md#^declaration-schema](../../../../specs/arch/testing-policy.md#^declaration-schema)): the assertions below prove the tab-strip side of the application-service/tab-strip-view handoff defined by [arch/ui/index.md](../../../../specs/arch/ui/index.md). Authored page labels, order, selection, and overflow states are fixtures. An implementation-mount hook supplies a fake application service and records page-selection callbacks; that service is a mock, greenlit here, and forfeits real application state, transport, and server behavior to [product/tab-pages.md](../../../../specs/product/tab-pages.md)'s acceptance assertions and [ui/app/stage/index.md](../../../../specs/ui/app/stage/index.md)'s selection and reorder coverage. Browser assertions whose claims do not involve motion use the standing motion override; that mock forfeits CSS motion to stage navigation and [#^tb-ac-drag-scroll](#^tb-ac-drag-scroll).

- **Contract** (the production tab-strip view, as the consumer side of the handoff from application and transient drag state to rendered markup, exercised in a mocked DOM; authored empty, selected, and carried tab lists are fixtures; the fake application service and direct posing of carried state are mocks, greenlit here, and forfeit real state transitions to the owners named above; the mocked DOM forfeits browser layout and interaction to the real-browser assertions below; no styling or motion claim): one table-driven markup smoke check covers an empty strip, one tab, several tabs with a middle selection, and one carried tab. The tablist renders one `role="tab"` control per page in order with its complete label and no icon or child control; a nonempty strip has exactly one `aria-selected="true"` tab, with no `data-active` or `aria-pressed` duplicate; that tab alone has `tabindex="0"` and every other tab has `tabindex="-1"`; the carried state places one `aria-hidden` placeholder immediately before its `.dragged` tab — *(covered by `packages/web/src/views/tab-strip.test.ts`; “empty strip”, “one tab”, “several tabs with a middle selection”, and “one carried tab”)*. ^tb-ac-markup-smoke
- **Contract** (the shared real-browser boundary, fixtures, service mock, and implementation-mount hook above, run in Chromium and Firefox; the exact fourteen GTD labels are fixtures; the default motion override is the only additional mock): with the strip under enough pressure to overflow, every tab is classified from untruncated text as exactly one of `capped` or `hugging` and carries no inline minimum width. Every capped tab occupies the compression floor and truncates; every hugging tab occupies its natural text-plus-chrome width and does not truncate. All tabs retain the same classification, width, and fractional-geometry truncation state through 120 consecutive animation frames without classification-attribute writes — *(covered by test: `packages/web/test/e2e/tab-strip.test.ts` “keeps GTD compression and truncation stable for 120 frames”)*. ^tb-ac-compression-stability
- **Contract** (the shared real-browser boundary, fixtures, service mock, and implementation-mount hook above, in Chromium and Firefox; authored short and long titles, container widths, and font faces are fixtures; native font loading and browser layout remain real; the default motion override is the only additional mock): the label's overflow marker follows fitting-to-overflowing transitions and their reversals after title updates, available-width changes, theme font-metric changes and subsequent font loads. Selected, unselected and hovered tabs in light and dark appearance retain the complete text and accessible name throughout. The marker is the behavioral input to the specified label mask; mask appearance remains subject to stylesheet conformance and visual review, not pixel assertions. Synchronization waits for view completion, actual font-load completion and observable overflow state, without wall-clock sleeps — *(covered by `packages/web/test/e2e/tab-strip.test.ts`, “tab label overflow”)*. ^tb-ac-label-overflow
- **Contract** (the shared real-browser boundary, fixtures, service mock, and implementation-mount hook above; the default motion override is the only additional mock and does not disable smooth scrolling; synchronization starts `scroll` and `scrollend` recorders before each scroll, waits for `scrollend` and two consecutive animation-frame samples with unchanged `scrollLeft`, and, after replacing the list, waits for view completion, `ResizeObserver` delivery, and the same stable frame pair): with enough tabs to overflow, the strip carries `data-overflow`; scrolling through an interior position and then to the far end advances the strip without changing that overflow state. Replacing the list with one that fits clears `data-overflow` — *(covered by test: `packages/web/test/e2e/tab-strip.test.ts` “marks overflow throughout the scroll range and clears it when the list fits”)*. ^tb-ac-scroll
- **Contract** (real-motion declaration: all animation and transition overrides are absent; the same boundary and setup, with the fake service acknowledging selection changes with production-shaped selected state; scroll and animation-frame recorders start before each selection, sample `scrollLeft` against animation-frame timestamps, and finish only after two consecutive frames with unchanged `scrollLeft`, never wall-clock sleeps): changing selection scrolls the strip to the selected tab's centred position — clamped at the band's ends for the first and last tab — over the crossing duration ([ui/app/stage/measures.yml#crossing.duration_ms](../../../../specs/ui/app/stage/measures.yml#crossing.duration_ms)), keeping its neighbours in view. The motion runs and completes rather than jumping, and the settled position holds through two further frames — *(covered by test: `packages/web/test/e2e/tab-strip.test.ts` “moves an interior selection over the crossing duration, clamps its ends, and holds settled”)*. ^tb-ac-selected-centres
- **Contract** (the same boundary and setup, with a middle tab selected and focusable controls before and after the strip; the fake service records any selection callback; the default motion override is the only additional mock): tabbing into the strip focuses its selected tab; unmodified Left and Right each leave that tab focused and selected and record no page-selection callback; the next Tab leaves the strip without visiting the other tabs — *(covered by test: `packages/web/test/e2e/tab-strip.test.ts` “tab-strip keyboard movement (^tb-ac-keyboard) > keeps selection and focus on the selected tab for plain arrows and leaves in one Tab”)*. ^tb-ac-keyboard
- **Contract** (real-motion declaration: all animation and transition overrides are absent; the production tab-reorder path runs in a real browser with authored overflowing and fitting tab lists, real pointer input holds the carried tab, and the fake application service forfeits persisted and shared reorder outcomes to [product/tab-pages.md#^tp-ac-reorder-shared](../../../product/tab-pages.md#^tp-ac-reorder-shared); synchronization installs a `scroll` recorder before entering an edge, samples `scrollLeft` against real animation-frame timestamps at each edge-zone probe, waits on animation frames for the placeholder to cross an initially offscreen neighbour, and retains sampling through stationary frames at each no-scroll probe, never using wall-clock sleeps; one release-race case controls when the view's one-shot font-settlement promise and its requested layout frame complete, while another changes the measured strip width after release and waits for real `ResizeObserver` delivery): with scroll range available in the tested direction, holding the tab at either horizontal edge scrolls that way so reorder positions outside the visible band become reachable; at the outer edge the sampled rate is [drag.yml#autoscroll.speed_px_s](../../../../specs/ui/app/drag.yml#autoscroll.speed_px_s), then tapers toward zero across [drag.yml#autoscroll.zone_px](../../../../specs/ui/app/drag.yml#autoscroll.zone_px) and is zero at and beyond the zone's inner boundary. No layout pass recentres while the gesture is dragging. An ordinary layout-centre request made during a drag is discarded; a selected-artifact change is instead deferred to the first layout pass after the gesture ends and outranks preservation of the edge-scrolled position. A late layout delivery preserves the resulting position only when the edge loop actually moved it. Leaving the zone or releasing stops further scrolling. When the tab list fits, the same edge hold emits no scroll event and leaves `scrollLeft` and placement unchanged — *(covered by tests: `packages/web/test/e2e/tab-strip.test.ts` “cancels a live selection crossing when an edge drag takes over scrolling”, “tapers both edge velocities, tracks newly revealed slots, and stops outside or on release”, “drops an ordinary centre request latched during a plain drag”, “defers selection centring until edge and plain drags finish”, “preserves only actual edge movement across a late resize delivery”, and “emits no scroll and leaves scroll position unchanged when the tabs fit”)*. ^tb-ac-drag-scroll

- **Contract** (the real-browser view boundary above): an overflowing strip mounts the fade helper for both edges with the authored fade distance; each end of the scroll range clears the masks at that edge, and changing the tab list updates the managed items — [tab-strip integration](../../../../specs/arch/ui/overflow-fade.md#tab-strip-integration) *(covered by tests: `packages/web/test/e2e/tab-strip.test.ts` “fades a selected edge tab while both scroll edges have remaining content” and “marks overflow throughout the scroll range and clears it when the list fits”)*. Disconnecting the view disposes its helper: retained tabs immediately lose their mask marker and properties, and reattaching their DOM without the view followed by resizing, scrolling and a class change does not recreate them *(covered by test: `packages/web/test/e2e/tab-strip.test.ts` “disconnecting the view clears masks and stops helper updates”)*. ^tb-ac-overflow-fade

- **Contract** (the existing real-browser tab-strip fixture and fake application service; the motion-suppressed input is supplied as the shell supplies it during sidebar animation): widening an overflowing strip until it fits, then narrowing it again, updates the overflow marker in the same ResizeObserver delivery, before another animation frame. This checks resize scheduling, not perceived smoothness; shell integration remains covered by the app motion-reference acceptance — *(covered by test: `packages/web/test/e2e/tab-strip.test.ts` “updates overflow before paint during sidebar motion (^tb-ac-sidebar-motion-layout)”)*. ^tb-ac-sidebar-motion-layout

Coverage relationship: [ui/app/stage/index.md](../../../../specs/ui/app/stage/index.md) owns release-versus-drag selection, tab/page coupling, and reorder mechanics; [#^tb-ac-drag-scroll](#^tb-ac-drag-scroll) owns only the strip's edge-scroll response while that gesture is held. [product/tab-pages.md](../../../../specs/product/tab-pages.md) owns the real-browser/server outcomes for labels, selection, and shared order. [#^tb-ac-keyboard](#^tb-ac-keyboard) owns the strip's single tab-order stop and unmodified Left and Right behavior. [arch/ui/keyboard-navigation.md#^kbn-ac-one-operation](../../../arch/ui/keyboard-navigation.md#^kbn-ac-one-operation) proves that pointer tab selection and the app-wide navigation chord enter the same page-selection operation; [product keyboard-navigation acceptance](../../../product/keyboard-navigation.md#^tp-ac-chord-focused-tab-browser) proves the chord from a focused tab in a browser and [its Electron counterpart](../../../product/keyboard-navigation.md#^tp-ac-chord-focused-tab-electron) proves the desktop application. [ui/app/top-bar/index.md](../../../../specs/ui/app/top-bar/index.md) owns the strip's position and available band within the bar. These owners compose with the focused strip contracts above rather than repeating their full-stack paths. This surface orders no Seam or Acceptance assertion because it owns no boundary crossing of its own.
