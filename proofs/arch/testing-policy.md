# Testing policy — proof

Proves [specs/arch/testing-policy.md](../../specs/arch/testing-policy.md).

The policy governs authoring and execution discipline, which is checked by review of the contribution and its recorded validation. It orders no tests of its wording or of an agent’s compliance. Runtime enforcement belongs to the test-runner specs and their proofs; its pointer to the shared-branch workflow moves no runtime promise into this spec and orders no additional tests.
