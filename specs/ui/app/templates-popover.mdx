# Templates popover

The navbar's Templates button opens a popover for creating an artifact from an artifact template. Each story on this page is one state of the popover; its markup and styles are the spec for that state. This page states the behaviour the stories cannot show.

The stories and their stylesheet are in `storybook/spec/templates-popover/`. Together with this page they are the authority for the popover's interaction, markup and styling. Production popover styles are derived from that stylesheet by hand and checked against the stories by eye; no automated stylesheet-copy or conformance check applies yet. The [product spec](../../product/templates.md#^artifact-template-popover) owns the feature promise and [template architecture](../../arch/templates/index.md#^artifact-template-scan) owns the scan result. The `skill-selector` folder beside this page holds the retired skills popover's frame and content, which do not specify this surface; its `thumbnails/` are sample images the stories use.

## Opening

Opening the popover scans the templates folder again and shows every valid template as a tile in a grid, with its image and name. The search field is focused and empty, and no tile is highlighted. The popover is placed as the popover spec places any panel. Near the navbar's trailing end, there is not room to align its left edge with the button's, so its right edge aligns with the button's instead.

## Searching

Typing in the search field narrows the grid to templates whose name or description contains the text, ignoring case, in their usual order. The first match is highlighted, so Return creates it. Clearing the field shows every template again, with no tile highlighted.

## No templates

When the templates folder holds no templates, the grid is replaced by a message suggesting the person ask their agent to make one. When it holds only folders with errors, the message says there are no valid templates, above the box of errors.

## The highlight

One tile at a time can be highlighted. The arrow keys move the highlight through the grid: Left and Right step to the previous and next tile, and Up and Down to the tile above and below; with no tile highlighted, any arrow key highlights the first. The highlight stops at the edges of the grid. Moving the pointer over a tile moves the highlight to it, and typing moves it to the first match. Keyboard focus stays in the search field throughout, which announces the highlighted tile to assistive technology. While there is no grid, the search field controls nothing and announces no tile. The highlight is the menu's highlight, not a selection: nothing stays chosen.

<Canvas of={Stories.Highlighted} />

While a tile is being pressed, it shows the pressed tint.

<Canvas of={Stories.Pressed} />

Tiles are not draggable, and their images and names cannot be selected as text.

## Creating an artifact

Pressing Return creates an artifact from the highlighted template; clicking a tile creates one from that tile's template. The artifact is created in the focused channel, to the right of the tab selected in this browser, or as the channel's only page when it has none, and is then focused. The popover closes. If creation fails, the popover still closes and no error is shown; focus and the selected tab stay as they were. Return with no tile highlighted does nothing. Escape closes the popover without creating anything.

## Images

A template's image is `thumbnail.png` in its folder: optional, portrait, ideally 4:5, such as 400 by 500 pixels. The tile crops it to fill the card from the top. Its size never makes a template invalid, and it is not copied into the artifact. Without one, the tile shows a placeholder.

## Templates with errors

Template folders that are not valid templates are listed in a box below the grid, each with its folder name and error. They are not affected by the search and cannot be created from.
