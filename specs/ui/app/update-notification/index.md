*UI spec: the update notification — the navbar bell and the notice popover; its interaction, markup, and styling.*

# Update notification (UI)

A yellow bell in the navbar's trailing position shows while an update applies; it presents the active notice's markdown in a popover. The notice is a server update from the channel, the desktop self-update notice for a desktop update the app has downloaded, or the deprecated desktop upgrade recommendation, shown only to desktop apps installed from npm; one is selected under the feature's precedence rule. This directory owns the surface's interaction, markup, copy, and styling ([spec-ui.md](../../../spec-ui.md)). What an update *means* and the feature's promises are owned by [product/update-notifications.md](../../../product/update-notifications.md); channel mechanics and server-notice dismissal by [arch/updates/update-channel.md](../../../arch/updates/update-channel.md); desktop self-update notice selection, restart handoff and persistence by [arch/updates/desktop-self-update-notice.md](../../../arch/updates/desktop-self-update-notice.md); desktop recommendation selection and persistence by [arch/updates/desktop-upgrade-recommendation.md](../../../arch/updates/desktop-upgrade-recommendation.md).

The supporting artifacts, each authoritative:

The surface is [update-notification.frame](./update-notification.frame); its `kind` parameter is the state interface, and selects one real notice in one state: a server update with its copy-prompt button, idle or just copied; the desktop self-update notice with its restart button, ready or restarting; or the desktop upgrade recommendation. Its styling is the panel's interior and its actions row. The panel is always in the markup, paired to the bell by its `trigger` reference; openness is the popover's `open` state ([ui/foundation/popover/index.md](../../foundation/popover/index.md), Markup). The panel itself is the popover's, the bell's color is the alert intent's ([ui/foundation/button/index.md](../../foundation/button/index.md)), and the composed copy button owns its swap.
- [ui/app/update-notification/content.yml](./content.yml) — the surface's fixed strings and the fixed markdown bodies [`desktop_self_update_notice`](./content.yml#desktop_self_update_notice) and [`desktop_upgrade_recommendation`](./content.yml#desktop_upgrade_recommendation). Production does not import this spec artifact; source constants conform to the two bodies under their owners ([^desktop-self-update-notice-render](../../../arch/updates/desktop-self-update-notice.md#^desktop-self-update-notice-render), [^desktop-rec-render](../../../arch/updates/desktop-upgrade-recommendation.md#^desktop-rec-render)). The frame shows each body as the markdown pipeline renders it, so the template does not import those strings directly, a stated exception to [spec-ui.md](../../../spec-ui.md)'s usual imported-content shape.

Agent and human review assess whether the implementation conforms to these supporting artifacts ([spec-ui.md](../../../spec-ui.md)).

## Interaction

- The notice follows the shared popover placement and stays open until dismissed. It never closes automatically. The notice anchors to the bell however it was opened: a bell press and automatic presentation read identically ([ui/foundation/popover/index.md](../../foundation/popover/index.md), Trigger).
- Closing the presented notice **is** dismissing it: the *Later* button, clicking the bell while open, and Escape from within the notice all close it. Outside clicks deliberately do not — an announcement must not vanish by accident, which is `manual` in the popover's terms: light dismissal is off, so every closing above is this surface's own behaviour. The active notice kind remembers the dismissal through its architecture owner: [the channel version](../../../arch/updates/update-channel.md#^dismissal), [the downloaded desktop version](../../../arch/updates/desktop-self-update-notice.md#^desktop-self-update-notice-dismissal) or [the recommended desktop version](../../../arch/updates/desktop-upgrade-recommendation.md#^desktop-rec-dismissal). ^un-closing
- The bell is not itself dismissable; clicking it presents the notice, including a dismissed one — the way back. ^un-bell-represents
- Auto-presentation never moves focus: an unprompted notice must not steal the keyboard from what the user is doing.
- The notice is an announcement (`role="status"`): presenting it is read out by assistive technology, not entered.
- The complete notice body is ordinary readable text: it supports browser text selection for every notice kind, overriding the app shell's selection-off stance ([ui/app/index.md#selection](../index.md#selection)). ^un-body-selection
- **The notice composes the reusable copy button only for a copyable prompt.** A server notice carrying a prompt renders the button. This surface supplies the fixed **Copy upgrade prompt** label and exact prompt string to the reusable copy button ([ui/app/copy-button/index.md](../copy-button/index.md)), whose copy/check markup, fixed **Copied** confirmation, 1.4-second dwell, announcement, no-resize swap, and clipboard mechanics apply unchanged. A server notice without a prompt, and the desktop recommendation, which carries none, end their actions with *Later*. The desktop self-update notice carries no prompt either and ends its actions with its restart button. Closing the notice clears any live copied state, so reopening never carries a stale confirmation. Confirmed in [TV-584](https://linear.app/telepath-computer/issue/TV-584). ^un-copy-button
- **The desktop self-update notice adds a restart button.** It is the notice's primary action, after *Later*, labelled **Restart to update**. Pressing it hands the restart to the notice's owner once ([^desktop-self-update-notice-restart](../../../arch/updates/desktop-self-update-notice.md#^desktop-self-update-notice-restart)); the button then reads **Restarting…** and is disabled until the page unloads, and the notice stays open. The server notice and the recommendation have no restart button. ^un-restart-button

## Testing

Under [UI-suite scope](../../../arch/testing-policy.md#What a UI surface's suite is responsible for), the suite for this surface must cover a server notice with an idle copy prompt, the same notice with copied confirmation, a server notice without a prompt, the desktop self-update notice ready to restart and restarting, and the desktop upgrade recommendation. Each state must render one bell button and a manual `<tv-popover>` whose `trigger` attribute names that button. The bell must use the fixed **Update available** text for the accessible name and tooltip and must contain the notification icon. The popover must use `role="status"`. It must contain the supplied body verbatim, with Later as the first action. Each state with a prompt must render one default copy button with the fixed **Copy upgrade prompt** label and the exact supplied prompt. In each state without a prompt, Later must be the only action. The desktop upgrade recommendation must contain the authored heading, the statement that this npm-installed copy receives no more updates, and the authored download link, and Later must be its only action. The desktop self-update notice must contain the authored heading and the supplied version, with Later first and one primary restart button labelled **Restart to update** as its only other action; restarting, that button must read **Restarting…** and be disabled.

Real-browser coverage must show that automatic presentation leaves the currently focused control focused. It must also show that the notice remains open until the user closes it. An outside press must leave the notice open without dismissing it. Selecting Later, pressing the bell while the notice is open, and pressing Escape from a focused notice control must each close the notice and dismiss it exactly once. Pressing the bell while the notice is closed must present it without dismissing it. Pressing the desktop self-update notice's restart button must hand the restart to the owner exactly once and leave the notice open, restarting.

If a notice is closed and reopened before its copy button's confirmation timer expires, the reopened notice must clear **Copied** and restore **Copy upgrade prompt**.

Real-browser coverage must show that the complete body supports text selection for both a server update notice and the desktop upgrade recommendation.

Real-browser coverage must show that an automatically presented notice follows the shared below/left placement and window-edge flipping rules. The same coverage must show that presenting the notice from the bell after dismissal places it in the same position.


Under [The suite does not assert styling adherence](../../../arch/testing-policy.md#^ui-styling-out), this surface's suite makes no claim about the visual treatment of the bell, panel, or button; panel dimensions or colors; button width; or reflow during the copy confirmation.

Under [Tests are the validation mechanism](../../../arch/testing-policy.md#Tests are the validation mechanism), [update-channel architecture](../../../arch/updates/update-channel.md#Testing) owns rendering the server notice body through the standard markdown pipeline. It also owns relay, server-notice dismissal persistence, the conditions under which the server notice exists, and server-notice telemetry. [Desktop self-update notice architecture](../../../arch/updates/desktop-self-update-notice.md#Testing) owns rendering the desktop self-update notice through markdown, its selection, precedence and persistence, and what the restart does. [Desktop upgrade recommendation architecture](../../../arch/updates/desktop-upgrade-recommendation.md) owns rendering the recommendation through markdown, recommendation selection and persistence, precedence and lifetime, and the absence of server-notice telemetry. [Product update acceptance](../../../product/update-notifications.md#Testing) owns the real browser and Electron paths against a running server. The linked proofs establish the content and update state that reach the notification. They also establish what happens after the user dismisses either notice. This surface's suite does not repeat how update state is transported, how dismissals are persisted, or the real browser and Electron paths.

Under the same rule, [copy-button testing](../copy-button/index.md#Testing) owns the reusable copy button's clipboard writes, confirmation timing, and announcement. [The popover](../../foundation/popover/index.md) owns toggle, dismissal, focus, and placement behaviour. [Navbar testing](../top-bar/index.md#Testing) owns the notification's presence and placement in the controls cluster. The copy button and popover proofs cover the behavior described above. This surface's suite owns when its manual popover closes and when it remains open. It also owns the prompt and label passed to the copy button, and clearing the copied confirmation when the notice is closed and reopened.

## Decisions

- The *Later* button is the explicit close; closing already dismisses, and the bell is the way back. No outside-click dismissal (deliberate: closing stays an act, never an accident).
- Automatic presentation is one write of the popover's `open` state ([arch/ui/elements.md#tv-popover](../../../arch/ui/elements.md#tv-popover)): the panel anchors to the bell it names, so a person's press and an update's arrival read identically, to the eye and to assistive technology.
- Deliberately no positioning and almost no CSS: this surface lays out the panel's interior and actions; the bell's color is the alert intent's, panel chrome and placement are the popover's, the composed copy button owns its swap, and the bell's placement is the navbar's.
- Button labels are the surface's copy, never channel-supplied: the channel sends content (the notice body, the prompt text), not interface text. The channel's `promptButtonLabel` field remains valid on the wire (frozen core) and is not consumed.
- The body is pipeline output placed verbatim. The server body contract is [arch/updates/update-channel.md#^toast-render](../../../arch/updates/update-channel.md#^toast-render)'s; the fixed desktop bodies and their handoff are [content.yml#desktop_self_update_notice](./content.yml#desktop_self_update_notice) with [arch/updates/desktop-self-update-notice.md#^desktop-self-update-notice-render](../../../arch/updates/desktop-self-update-notice.md#^desktop-self-update-notice-render)'s, and [content.yml#desktop_upgrade_recommendation](./content.yml#desktop_upgrade_recommendation) with [arch/updates/desktop-upgrade-recommendation.md#^desktop-rec-render](../../../arch/updates/desktop-upgrade-recommendation.md#^desktop-rec-render)'s.
